Frequently asked questions

NotX Labs is a technology company based in Nigeria that builds software products to a global standard. Its products are Sparks, Qovu, Submitiv and Social AI Studio.

NotX Labs builds four products: Sparks, a messaging platform with voice and video calls; Qovu, a verified attendance system for lecturers; Submitiv, a deadline-enforced submission platform; and Social AI Studio, an approval-first AI social content tool that is in development.

Qovu is NotX Labs' verified attendance system for lecturers. The lecturer projects a QR code or shares a 6-digit passcode, and students check in with only their registration number — no student accounts. Check-ins are verified on the server with location checks and a live selfie check, and cross-checked against the lecturer's headcount.

Sparks is NotX Labs' messaging platform. It offers fast chats, voice notes, and built-in voice and video calls, with replies, reactions, forwarding and search inside every chat. Sparks is live at sparkes.vercel.app.

Submitiv is NotX Labs' deadline-controlled submission platform. You create a request and share one public link; anyone can submit without an account, each submission gets a unique reference and an emailed receipt, and the server enforces the deadline so late submissions are closed automatically.

No. Social AI Studio is in development. It will plan and draft platform-specific social content with AI, and nothing will be published without human approval. LinkedIn and Facebook Pages are planned first, with Instagram next.
